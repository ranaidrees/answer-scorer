Answer scorer demo

AI-assisted, decision support only

An AI model reads one written interview answer and drafts a score against a rubric, with a rationale and quotes from the answer. It does not decide anything about a candidate. A trained person makes the decision, and every low score or flagged answer is sent to a person for review.

This is a demonstration with synthetic data. Do not enter real candidate answers, names or contact details.

The token is kept in this browser tab only and is cleared when the tab closes.
